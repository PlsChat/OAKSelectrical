Make safe first, paperwork right behind it

The electrical side of a claim tends to sit on the critical path. Nothing else can start until the property is safe, and the claim can't settle until somebody has written down what the damage actually was. Both of those are our job.

We attend, isolate what needs isolating and make the installation safe. Then we produce a scope of works with photographs and an itemised price, so the adjuster is looking at evidence rather than an estimate. Where an EICR or condition report is needed to establish the pre-loss state, we issue that too.

What we handle

  • Emergency attendance, isolation and make-safe
  • Damage assessment and itemised scope of works
  • EICRs and condition reports for claims
  • Full and partial rewires
  • Consumer unit and distribution board replacement
  • Temporary supplies while a property is dried out
  • Photographic evidence and written reporting
  • Direct liaison with adjusters, brokers and contractors

What adjusters get from us

  • A written trail from day one

    First attendance, findings, scope, works, certificates. Every stage is documented so nothing has to be reconstructed from memory later.

  • Photographs that actually show the damage

    Taken before anything is disturbed, captioned and referenced against the scope of works.

  • An itemised scope, not a lump sum

    So the claim can be assessed line by line rather than argued over as a single figure.

  • One named contact

    The same engineer from first attendance to final certificate. No repeating the background to a different person each time.
