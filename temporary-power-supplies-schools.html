Temporary power supplies for schools

Temporary power is a speciality of ours, and schools are where we do most of it — nationwide. Refurbishments, extensions, modular classrooms, DNO changeovers and emergency loss of supply. We design it, install it, maintain it while it's in, and strip it out cleanly at the end.

A school can't lose its supply mid-term

Temporary power in a school is not the same job as temporary power on a building site. There are children on the other side of the hoarding, a kitchen that has to serve lunch, and a hall that might be an exam room in three weeks. The installation has to be safe against people who will lean on it, and reliable against a timetable that can't slip.

We survey early, price the whole thing including the strip-out, and programme the install into the days the school actually has — usually a half-term or the summer holidays. Long-lead items get ordered against that date rather than against a hopeful one.

What we supply

  • Temporary builders supplies and full site setups
  • Generator hook-ups, changeover panels and load banks
  • Modular and temporary classroom supplies
  • Hall, kitchen and exam-room power
  • 63A and 125A distribution, festoon and task lighting
  • Emergency response to loss of supply
  • Weekly inspections and RCD test records
  • Decommissioning, reinstatement and making good
