01 · Retail & commercial fit-out

The store opens at seven, whatever happened at two.

Most of our fit-out work happens in trading stores and occupied buildings. That means night shifts, phased isolations and a site left clean and safe for staff and customers in the morning. We've delivered refits and remodels for national supermarket chains, including Morrisons stores.

More on shop fit-out
  • Supermarket refits, remodels and new store builds
  • Shell fit-out, sub-mains and distribution boards
  • Switchgear, isolators and containment
  • LED lighting installation and upgrades
  • Emergency lighting installation and testing
  • Chiller, refrigeration and plant power
  • Tills, EPOS, data containment and comms cabling
  • Temporary lighting and power during phased works
  • Testing, certification and O&M handover

Working live. Every job is issued with RAMS before we start, isolations are agreed in writing with the store or site manager, and we work to whatever access window you set — nights, early mornings or full closures.

02 · Temporary power supplies

A school can't lose its supply mid-term.

Temporary power is a speciality of ours, and schools are where we do most of it — nationwide. Refurbishments, extensions, modular classrooms, DNO changeovers and emergency loss of supply. We design it, install it, maintain it while it's in, and strip it out cleanly at the end.

More on temporary power
  • Temporary builders supplies and full site setups
  • Generator hook-ups, changeover panels and load banks
  • Modular and temporary classroom supplies
  • Hall, kitchen and exam-room power
  • 63A and 125A distribution, festoon and task lighting
  • Emergency response to loss of supply
  • Weekly inspections and RCD test records
  • Decommissioning, reinstatement and making good

Planned to the academic calendar. Most school works land in half-terms and the summer holidays, so we survey early, pre-order long-lead items and programme the install to the days you actually have. Engineers on school sites are DBS checked.

03 · Insurance & reinstatement

Make safe first. Paperwork right behind it.

Fire, flood, impact and escape of water. We attend, isolate and make the property safe, then scope, price and carry out the reinstatement — with the reports, photographs and certificates loss adjusters and claims handlers need to settle.

More on insurance work
  • Emergency attendance, isolation and make-safe
  • Damage assessment and itemised scope of works
  • EICRs and condition reports for claims
  • Full and partial rewires
  • Consumer unit and distribution board replacement
  • Temporary supplies while a property is dried out
  • Photographic evidence and written reporting
  • Direct liaison with adjusters, brokers and contractors

One point of contact. You get a named engineer on the job and a written trail from first attendance to final certificate, so the claim isn't held up waiting on electrical evidence.
